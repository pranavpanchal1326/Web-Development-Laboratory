Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the battery last?

The Aura earbuds provide 8 hours of continuous playback on a single charge. With the charging case, you get an additional 22 hours, totaling 30 hours of listening time. Quick charge feature gives you 3 hours of playback with just 15 minutes of charging.

Is it compatible with iPhone and Android?

Yes! Aura earbuds are fully compatible with iPhone (iOS 10+), Android (5.0+), Windows, and macOS. We also have a dedicated Aura Connect app available on both App Store and Google Play Store for advanced customization.

Can I use them for workouts and swimming?

Absolutely! The earbuds have IPX7 water resistance rating, making them perfect for workouts, running in rain, and heavy sweating. However, they're not designed for swimming or full submersion in water.

What's your return policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied, return the product in original condition for a full refund. We also provide a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects.

How effective is the noise cancellation?

Our AI-powered adaptive noise cancellation can reduce ambient noise by up to 35dB. It automatically adjusts to your environment - whether you're on a plane, in a café, or on a busy street. You can also customize the level through our mobile app.
